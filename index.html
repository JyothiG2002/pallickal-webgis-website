About Pallickal Grama Panchayat

Pallickal Grama Panchayat is a local self-government institution situated in Pathanamthitta District, Kerala. It plays an important role in rural administration, infrastructure development, public service delivery, and community welfare. The Panchayat is part of the Parakode Block Panchayat and Adoor Taluk. It includes the villages of Pallickal and Peringanad. The region represents the rural landscape of Kerala, with settlements, agricultural areas, roads, water resources, and other important geographical features. This Interactive WebGIS Platform has been developed to provide a spatial view of Pallickal Grama Panchayat. It helps users explore geographical information and understand the spatial distribution of different features within the Panchayat.

Administrative Boundaries and Location

Pallickal Grama Panchayat is located in Adoor Taluk of Pathanamthitta District, Kerala. It comes under Parakode Block Panchayat. The Panchayat comprises Pallickal and Peringanad villages. Its geographical extent covers approximately 45.43 square kilometres.
